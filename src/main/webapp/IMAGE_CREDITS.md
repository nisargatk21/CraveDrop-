# CraveDrop image credits

All restaurant covers, menu-card photos, and site food backgrounds are bundled locally under `assets/`; the app does not hotlink images at runtime. Any third-party photographs are credited individually in the table below with their source, creator and license. All other site, restaurant and menu photographs are original AI-generated assets bundled locally with the project.

`assets/site/hero-v2.jpg` is an original AI-generated editorial food photograph created for CraveDrop. It contains no third-party logo or brand mark.

All 30 files under `assets/restaurants/` are original AI-generated, brand-free restaurant cover photos created for CraveDrop. Each distinct photo is paired to the restaurant named by its matching row in `src/main/resources/catalog/restaurants.tsv`. They are bundled locally and do not require third-party image attribution.

All 180 files under `assets/menu/items/` are original AI-generated, brand-free menu photos created for CraveDrop. Each file shows its named dish and is mapped by `src/main/resources/catalog/menu-items.tsv`. They are bundled locally and do not require third-party image attribution.
